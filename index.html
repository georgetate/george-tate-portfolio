My Photography Aug 2025 - Continued

The photography style I've gravitated to is close up framing with deep shadows. It's interesting to me because it leaves so much up to interpretation and personal creativity. While close ups are my favorite, they have caused me to struggle finding exciting medium and wide shots.

Oftentimes little edited pictures of the surroundings pop into my head while I am driving or walking. Highway overpasses, cracks in the sidewalk, and afternoon shadows are all seen from a new and enlivened angle. For me, photography's joy originates from how it forces a fresh perspective and improves my imagination.

Honda Christmas Special Restoration Jul - Dec 2025

I restored this collectible Honda mini-bike with a friend from church. This entailed cleaning the carb, other engine work, and some extensive detailing. Easily the most involved part was removing all the rust from the inside of the gas tank. The first ride made all the work worth it.

This mini-bike was originally my Grandpa's. After sitting in the shed for decades, I got it from my Grandma. I was planning on auctioning it off in order to fund future projects. But right before listing the bike my Grandma passed, so I decided to keep it in remembrance of both her and my Grandpa. Now it's an heirloom that I'll ride with my own kids.

Soccer Jersey Prom-posal Apr 2025

I was hoping to make something striking with a clear subject or action, like a bicycle kick. After a few tests, I quickly realized how difficult it is to paint details on textile. I would have to settle on something much simpler. This was initially disappointing because the result didn't live up to my expectations, but it quickly started to grow on me. I'm even a little surprised about how much I like it now.

The mural and signatures were for a prom proposal during my senior year. Stadium lights, soccer, how my friends were part of it, and of course the over the top shirt all make for a fun memory. I think the best parts of this experience are the cool stories I get to tell and new friends I met during the process.

From Scratch Coffee Table Jan - Mar 2025

This was particularly time consuming because I didn't have access to quality wood or a workshop. Both the hand tools and the knotty wood made it pretty difficult to get straight cuts. Though, there was something enjoyable about sitting on the floor of my room and cleaning sawdust out of the carpet.

Building this table really tested my patience, critical thinking, and resourcefulness, which is why it's special to me. It's evidence that I can work around poor quality tools, bad materials, and inexperience. It's something physical I can look back at when tackling future challenges.
