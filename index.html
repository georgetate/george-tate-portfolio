My Photography Aug 2025 – Continued

My favorite part about building this portfolio is how photography has developed my imagination. While driving or walking, little edited pictures of the surroundings pop into my head. Highway overpasses, cracks in the sidewalk, and afternoon shadows are all seen from a new and enlivened angle. For me, photography's joy orginates from how it forces a fresh perspective.

Honda Christmas Special Restoration Jul – Dec 2025

This mini-bike was originally my Grandpa's. After sitting in the shed for decades, I got it from my Grandma. I was planning on auctioning it off inorder to fund future projects. Right before listing the bike circumstances majorly changed, so I decided to keep it in rememberance of my grandparents. One day I hope to ride it around with my own family.
